## Cross-screen system (what to extract as reusable tokens)

- **Backgrounds**: one flat, muted-but-saturated color per screen/section, never white, never gradient. Orange, lime-green, pink used here; extend the same saturation/muted level to the app's own team colors rather than using neon versions.
- **Cards**: white or cream fill, solid black border 2-3px, rounded corners in the 20-24px range (not sharp, not fully rounded — a consistent "chunky but soft" radius is the signature), hard offset drop shadow with zero blur on the more prominent/interactive cards, no shadow on flatter background-colored cards (Screen 1's colored cards have no shadow; Screen 2's white cards do — shadow presence tracks with "is this a raised, tappable surface" vs "is this a colored content block").
- **Buttons/pills**: fully rounded (capsule) for small actions and filters, black text on white or on a flat accent color, thin black border, no shadow on these small pills.
- **Icon buttons**: circular, white or accent-colored fill, thin black border, single black or white glyph centered, used for back/menu/utility actions in headers.
- **Bottom nav pattern**: a floating black capsule bar (not edge-to-edge, not a plain white bar) with white icon glyphs for inactive items and a white pill-within-the-bar for the active item (either icon+label or label-only). This is a strong, repeatable signature element worth reusing exactly as-is.
- **Illustrated characters**: thick uniform black outline, flat cel-shaded color fill (2-3 tones max per character, no gradients or soft shading), personified food/object mascots with simple cartoon limbs and faces, small motion/sparkle marks for energy, used as the single decorative/emotional focal point per screen rather than scattered small icons.
- **Typography**: one bold, rounded/geometric display face for headlines and big numbers (very heavy weight, tight tracking), same face at regular weight for labels/captions, numbers get outsized treatment versus surrounding text (the "90,744" and dollar figures are the largest type on their screens after the page title).
- **Color relationships**: each screen's accent/highlight color (the orange chart bar, the green "Year" pill, the yellow/red header buttons) borrows from the *other* screens' background colors — the palette is shared and cross-pollinated across screens rather than each screen being visually isolated.
